sections:
  - title: "Three-layer architecture"
    items:
      - "Fixed, PTZ or 360-degree IP cameras capture relevant locations."
      - "NVIDIA Jetson AI Boxes process video at the edge and publish events through MQTT/TLS or webhooks."
      - "A central VMS presents cameras, maps and alerts; drone feeds can be integrated to verify incidents."
  - title: "Hardware and edge processing"
    intro: "Select the configuration based on camera count, algorithm mix and site survey findings."
    items:
      - "Jetson Orin NX 16GB Super is the primary box option; Orin Nano Super may suit smaller clusters."
      - "DeepStream handles the RTSP, video decoding, TensorRT inference and object-tracking pipeline."
      - "About 16 channels of 1080p decoding per box is a design estimate. Actual AI throughput depends on algorithm workload and requires on-site benchmarking."
  - title: "Algorithms selected for each use case"
    intro: "The catalog contains 21 algorithms across asset protection, fire safety, public order, access control, intrusion and investigation. Not every algorithm runs simultaneously on every configuration."
    items:
      - "Asset protection: excavation, camera tampering, left/removed objects, vandalism and scene changes."
      - "Safety and public order: fire/smoke, audio events, fights, policy violations and unusual crowds."
      - "Access control: people counting, vehicle tracking, Vietnamese plate recognition, virtual tripwires and face lists."
      - "Intrusion and investigation: night intrusion, animals, unauthorized drones, PTZ tracking and weapons."
      - "Three platform capabilities: analytics dashboard, on-site deterrence and smart video search."
  - title: "Match channel count to workload"
    intro: "Channel counts are design estimates, not a promise that every channel runs all 21 algorithms. Benchmark mAP and latency with the intended workload before confirming a configuration."
    items:
      - "Light workloads such as detecting and tracking people, vehicles or animals: up to an estimated 16 channels per box."
      - "Medium workloads with fire/smoke, tampering, tripwire or plate recognition: around 8–12 channels per box."
      - "Heavy workloads with pose, actions, face recognition or several combined algorithms: around 2–6 channels per box."
  - title: "Packages by sector"
    items:
      - "Heritage sites: prioritize excavation, night intrusion, fire/smoke, animals and artifact-store protection."
      - "Forestry and rangers: monitor fire, intrusion and patrol routes based on risk zones."
      - "Industrial sites and warehouses: focus on intrusion, fire, access and operational safety."
faq:

  - question: "How many cameras can each box support?"
    answer: "It depends on resolution and algorithm workload. Sixteen channels is a decoding estimate; AI throughput must be benchmarked on site before final configuration."
