features:
  - "Daily operations overview — a time-based landing screen with guest, order, revenue and open-complaint figures, plus upcoming reservations and this week's birthdays."
  - "360° customer profile — a guest list filterable by membership tier and messaging-channel interest; each profile shows visits, last visit, total spend, loyalty points, order history and care history."
  - "Reservation management by branch — reservations listed and calendared by time range and status, following a clear cycle (booked → seated → completed / no-show / cancelled)."
  - "Order management & revenue reconciliation — orders by branch and sales channel with a full status lifecycle from placement to payment or cancellation, supporting reconciliation and revenue reporting."
benefits:
  - "Recognise regulars and bring them back at the right time, instead of letting guest data drift after each bill."
  - "See revenue, orders and reservation activity across branches on one screen."
useCases:
  - "Restaurant chains with 3–20 branches and high daily transaction volumes that need POS customer data consolidated in one place for follow-up."
  - "F&B chains that want to retain regulars: identify loyal guests, track the most recent visit and reach out at the right moment."
  - "Chains already using Zalo OA for customer care: know which guests linked their messaging account and how engaged they are."
  - "Chains that manage table reservations: track reservations per branch without missing guests."
  - "Chain management that needs one dashboard for revenue, orders and complaints across branches."

    items:

      - "Reservations and orders are organised per branch with a clear status lifecycle (booked → seated → completed / no-show / cancelled)."

  - title: "Connected to the systems you already run"
    intro: "The difference is that the system sits inside the operating chain and connects to outside systems, rather than asking staff to re-enter data."
    items:

      - "Zalo OA: links customer accounts, measures engagement and pushes marketing notifications."
      - "Call centre: records customer-care calls."
faq:
  - question: "Where does customer data come from?"
    answer: "The system takes data from the chain's POS and links it to the Zalo OA channel, rather than requiring manual entry."
  - question: "Do we need to enter customers from scratch?"
    answer: "No. Customers are synced from the sales system, so profiles already include visit history and past purchases."
  - question: "Does it suit multi-branch chains?"
    answer: "Yes. Orders and reservations are kept per branch, supporting reconciliation and revenue reporting across branches."
  - question: "Does it support customer care over messaging channels?"
    answer: "It links to Zalo OA to measure engagement and send notifications; more channels can be added as needed."
