Algorithms are grouped into packages; DJI Dock 2 and Matrice 3TD would complement patrols, thermal observation and controlled event verification."
result: "This is a proposal, not a confirmed deployment outcome. Expected results include improved event detection and verification, less reliance on streaming all video to the center and support for patrols in priority areas. The preliminary estimate and timeline are discussion inputs; configuration, cost and schedule require a site survey, approval and quotations."
